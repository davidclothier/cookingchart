# Trademark policy · Política de marca

🇬🇧 The code is open source (Apache 2.0) and the specification is open (CC BY 4.0). The names **"Cooking Chart"** and **"CCL"** and the logo are not covered by those licenses. They identify the official project, so people know what they are getting.

**You may, without asking:**
- Say that your software, plugin or site *uses*, *supports* or *is compatible with* Cooking Chart / CCL, as long as it passes the conformance suite (`node conformance/run.js` or an equivalent run of `conformance/cases/`).
- Use the names to refer to the project in articles, talks, papers and teaching.
- Keep the "Made with Cooking Chart" link in embedded charts, or remove it.

**Please ask first before:**
- Using the names or logo in the name of a product, company, app or domain.
- Publishing a modified specification under the same name. Extensions are welcome as vocabulary packs (SPEC §9) or as enhancement proposals to the official specification.
- Implying endorsement or affiliation.
